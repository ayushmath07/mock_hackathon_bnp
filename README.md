In the context of the BNP Paribas Innoversité Hackathon (hosted by BNP Paribas India Solutions / India CoE across Bengaluru, Mumbai, and Chennai on HackerEarth and their Innoversité Portal), UC 3: AltCredit addresses a critical challenge in modern retail and corporate banking: Alternative Credit Risk Assessment and Financial Scoring Models.1. Problem Statement Overview: The Core ChallengeThe Banking ContextTraditional credit underwriting frameworks (such as FICO, CIBIL, or Experian) evaluate borrowers based on historical credit bureau footprints (past loan repayments, existing credit card limits, mortgages, and formal debt history).The Blind SpotThis approach creates a significant financial inclusion barrier for:"Thin-File" & "New-to-Credit" (NTC) borrowers: Young professionals, college graduates, and immigrants.Gig Economy & Informal Sector Workers: Freelancers, delivery partners, and contractors with fluctuating, non-salaried income.Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises (MSMEs): Businesses operating largely on cash flow, digital invoices, and UPI/POS turnover rather than audited balance sheets.The Objective of AltCreditTo design and build an intelligent, fair, and regulatory-compliant alternative credit assessment system. It harnesses non-traditional financial and digital footprint data to compute a borrower’s Probability of Default (PD) and generate a standardized Alternative Credit Score, allowing banks to extend credit safely without inflating Non-Performing Assets (NPAs).2. What Data Sources Are Analyzed?An effective AltCredit solution processes multi-dimensional, non-traditional signals:Cash Flow & Banking Activity (Open Banking / Account Aggregator):Net cash inflows/outflows, average daily balance, and recurring transaction regularity.Cheque bounces, overdraft usage, and emergency liquidity buffers.Utility & Recurring Payment Footprints:Timeliness of electricity, water, telecom/broadband, and rent payments.Commerce & Digital Footprint:E-commerce purchase consistency, returns/refund patterns, and reliance on Buy-Now-Pay-Later (BNPL) vs. debit/prepaid.MSME Business Cash Flows (if assessing small enterprises):GST return filing regularity, invoice payment cycles, vendor concentration, and POS/merchant UPI transaction volumes.3. A. Feature Engineering PipelineTransform raw transactional records into financial behavior indicators:Income Stability Index: Variance and frequency of monthly deposits.Expense-to-Income & Discretionary Spend Ratio: Proportion of income allocated to non-essential spending.Payment Reliability Score: Punctuality rate of utility bills, mobile recharges, and subscriptions.Liquidity Buffer Days: Number of days living expenses can be covered by typical cash balances.B. Machine Learning Scoring EngineModel Choices:Gradient Boosted Trees (LightGBM / XGBoost / CatBoost) for tabular data.Scorecard approach (Logistic Regression with Weight of Evidence (WoE) and Information Value (IV)) for strict banking compliance.Score Calibration: Map the predicted Probability of Default ($PD \in [0, 1]$) to a credit score scale (e.g., 300 to 850 or 0 to 1000), categorized into risk tiers (Prime, Near-Prime, Sub-Prime).C. Explainable AI (XAI) & Regulatory GovernanceBanking regulations (such as Basel III/IV, RBI guidelines, and GDPR) prohibit black-box credit decisions.Integrate SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) or LIME to output Adverse Action Reason Codes (e.g., "Score penalised by 45 points due to high income volatility and late utility bill settlement").Run a basic fairness/bias check to verify the model does not discriminate against demographic or proxy attributes.D. Decision & Policy EngineFormulate credit policy rules:Score $\ge$ Threshold: Auto-approve with dynamic risk-adjusted credit limit and APR.Marginal Band: Flag for manual underwriter review.Below Cutoff: Reject with explicit reasons and actionable tips to improve credit health.E. User Interfaces / PortalsRisk Officer / Underwriter Dashboard:Applicant summary, alternative credit score, risk tiering, cash flow trend charts, and SHAP feature importance breakdown.Borrower Transparency View:Consent-driven data sharing simulator, credit score display, and personalized financial tips to improve credit standing.4.
